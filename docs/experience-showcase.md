# Enterprise Experience Showcase

> Candidate input required before submission. Replace each bracketed prompt with a truthful 1–2 paragraph example. Use Situation → Decision/Action → Measurable Result. Do not submit invented metrics or responsibilities.

## 1. Financial or ERP System and Business Impact

**System and role:** [What financial/ERP platform did you build or significantly contribute to? What were you personally accountable for?]

**Problem and scale:** [Users, tenants/entities, transaction volume, countries/currencies, or business process affected.]

**Technical contribution:** [The architecture or difficult decision you owned.]

**Business impact:** [A truthful measurable result: close time, processing latency, error reduction, availability, cost, revenue, or user adoption.]

## 2. Data Integrity or Reconciliation Issue

**Failure observed:** [What did not reconcile? How was it detected?]

**Root cause:** [Race condition, duplicate event, rounding, stale data, missing transaction boundary, bad mapping, or another concrete cause.]

**Resolution:** [Control or design change you introduced—idempotency, constraint, reconciliation job, transaction boundary, alert, or repair process.]

**Evidence:** [How you proved the repair and prevented recurrence.]

## 3. Period Close, Audit, or Compliance

**Context:** [Month/year-end close, external audit, SOX/control review, tax filing, or another regulated process.]

**Control challenge:** [Late postings, approval evidence, audit lineage, locked periods, segregation of duties, retention, or access review.]

**Your action:** [Process and system changes you personally drove.]

**Outcome:** [Audit result, close-time reduction, control finding closed, or operational improvement.]

## 4. Multi-Tenant or Multi-Entity Data Modeling

**Context:** [A system where one customer/tenant contained sites, subsidiaries, business units, or another hierarchy.]

**Hard problem:** [Isolation, shared reference data, hierarchy queries, authorization, noisy-neighbor control, migrations, or reporting.]

**Design:** [Tenant keys, entity hierarchy, authorization boundary, database strategy, and operational trade-off.]

**Result:** [Scale achieved, incident avoided, onboarding improvement, query reduction, or another truthful outcome.]

## Interview Bridge to This Prototype

After completing the examples, close with two or three sentences connecting the experience to this assessment. For example: explain how a prior tenant/site/device hierarchy informed the Tenant → Entity → Invoice design, or how a reconciliation incident informed the idempotency, audit, and AR-to-GL controls here.
